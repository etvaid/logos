# Forensic in LOGOS SPECTACULAR: A Step-by-Step Tutorial

## Overview
Forensic is a powerful feature in LOGOS SPECTACULAR designed to enhance data analysis, research, and detailed examination of resources within the software. It allows users to scrutinize texts, compare sources, and analyze context, making it a valuable tool for researchers, scholars, and students.

## Prerequisites
Before you start using Forensic in LOGOS SPECTACULAR, ensure you have:
1. A valid subscription or access to LOGOS SPECTACULAR.
2. Basic familiarity with the interface of LOGOS SPECTACULAR.
3. Research materials or texts you wish to analyze.
4. Completed tutorials related to basic functions of LOGOS SPECTACULAR.

## Step 1: Accessing Forensic
1. **Open LOGOS SPECTACULAR**: Launch the application and log in with your credentials.
2. **Locate the Forensic Tool**: In the main menu or dashboard, look for the "Forensic" feature. This may be located under a ‘Research Tools’ or ‘Analysis’ section.
3. **Click to Open**: Select the Forensic option to launch it in a new window.

### Example:
You’ve logged in and found the Forensic section under "Advanced Tools". Click on it to begin.

---

## Step 2: Selecting Text for Analysis
1. **Choose Your Resource**: Use the resource selection panel to choose the text or dataset you want to analyze.
2. **Specify Criteria**: If applicable, specify the criteria for analysis (e.g., specific chapters, verses, or sections).
   
### Example:
Select "Biblical Texts" and then choose the Book of Genesis for your analysis.

---

## Step 3: Setting Up Forensic Parameters
1. **Define Parameters**: Determine what aspects of the text you want to focus on - themes, keywords, phrase occurrences, etc.
2. **Utilize Filters**: Apply filters to narrow down your results (e.g., timeframe, authorship).

### Example:
You want to analyze themes of creation in Genesis. Apply a filter for keywords like "creation," "light," and "earth."

---

## Step 4: Conducting the Analysis
1. **Run the Analysis**: Hit the ‘Analyze’ button to execute your forensic examination based on your selected text and defined parameters.
2. **Review Results**: Observe the output, which may include highlighted keywords, frequency data, or comparative analyses with other texts.

### Example:
After execution, a report appears displaying the frequency of the term "creation" across various chapters in Genesis, along with similar references in the Book of John.

---

## Step 5: Interpreting and Exporting Findings
1. **Analyze Results**: Look through the findings. Use graphs or charts if available for better visualization.
2. **Document Findings**: Take notes on key insights gathered during the analysis.
3. **Export Options**: If you wish to keep the results for future reference, choose to export the data in your preferred format (PDF, Excel, etc.).

### Example:
Interpret the high frequency of "creation" in chapters 1-2 of Genesis. Export the data chart showing the comparison with the Book of John into a PDF for your research paper.

---

## Summary
In this tutorial, you learned how to navigate and utilize the Forensic feature in LOGOS SPECTACULAR. You accessed the tool, selected resources, set up parameters, conducted your analysis, and interpreted results. This process enhances your data analysis capabilities, equipping you for in-depth research.
