## 2. Getting Started

### 2.1 System Requirements

To run LOGOS SPECTACULAR smoothly, ensure your system meets the following requirements:

- **Operating System**: Windows (10 or later), macOS (10.14 or later), or Linux (Ubuntu 18.04 or later)
- **RAM**: Minimum 8 GB (16 GB recommended)
- **Processor**: Intel i5 or equivalent (i7 or better recommended)
- **Internet Connection**: Required for initial setup and updates
- **Browser**: Latest version of Chrome, Firefox, or Safari

### 2.2 Installation

1. **Download**: Visit the official LOGOS SPECTACULAR website and download the installation package for your operating system.
   
2. **Run Installer**: Open the downloaded file and follow the prompts in the setup wizard.
   
3. **Permissions**: Grant necessary permissions for installation. 

4. **Completion**: Once installed, launch the application by clicking its icon in your applications folder or desktop.

### 2.3 User Registration

Upon launching for the first time:

1. **Sign Up**: Create your user account by providing your email address and setting a secure password. 
   
2. **Verification**: Check your email for a verification link. Click the link to confirm your account.

3. **Login**: Return to the platform, enter your credentials, and sign in.

## 3. Features Overview

### 3.1 Semantia

**Semantia** enables semantic analysis of classical texts through cutting-edge natural language processing techniques. This feature allows users to understand meaning, context, and usage of words, phrases, and entire sentences.

#### Example Usage

- **Disambiguation**: Input the Greek word "ἀρχή" (archê) and observe how Semantia identifies its meanings in different contexts – it could refer to 'beginning', 'principality', or 'authority'.

- **Contextual Insights**: By analyzing the word's occurrences in various texts, users can see how meanings evolve depending on literary sources.

### 3.2 Chronos

**Chronos** tracks and illustrates temporal word drift, showcasing how the meanings and usages of words have changed over different periods. 

#### Example Usage

- **Temporal Maps**: Enter the Latin word "mensa" and visually navigate through its semantic shifts from ancient to modern times, helping scholars understand historical linguistics and cultural change.

- **Historical Comparisons**: Compare how specific words were used in texts from different centuries, enhancing your understanding of socio-historical contexts.

### 3.3 Reader

The **Reader** feature enhances the experience of reading texts by providing integrated tools for annotation, bookmarking, and personalized libraries.

#### Example Usage

1. **Annotation**: Highlight a passage from Homer's "Iliad" and add notes directly within the platform. These annotations can be saved and exported later.
   
2. **Bookmarking**: Create a personalized study path by bookmarking critical passages you want to revisit.

3. **Library Management**: Organize your texts into folders based on themes, authors, or periods.

### 3.4 Discovery

**Discovery** utilizes AI to facilitate research, offering tailored recommendations and insights drawn from the user's previous engagements on the platform.

#### Example Usage

- **Personalized Recommendations**: Based on your previous readings, Discovery may suggest relevant texts or scholarly articles, making your research journey simpler and faster.

- **Research Connections**: View AI-generated connections between texts by lineage and influence. For example, connecting Plato's works with subsequent philosophers can yield exciting insights.

### 3.5 Connectome

**Connectome** provides a comprehensive map of connections between over 500,000 entities, encompassing people, texts, and concepts in classical literature.

#### Example Usage

- **Entity Exploration**: Search for figures such as Aristotle and discover all texts and concepts related to his works. Tap on hyperlinks to dive into related discussions and entities.

- **Mapping Influence Networks**: Visual representations allow users to explore which works or figures influenced others in the classical tradition.

### 3.6 Translation

**Translation** leverages AI and expert translations to deliver high-quality translations of classical texts while providing options for textual comparison.

#### Example Usage

- **Dynamic Translation**: Enter a line from Catullus and receive an interactive translation that allows you to toggle between the original Latin and various translations. 

- **Comparative Analysis**: Analyze and juxtapose different versions of a translation side by side, evaluating nuances in meanings and choices made by translators.

### 3.7 Forensic

The **Forensic** tool applies stylometry to analyze authorship attribution and textual consistency across classical literature.

#### Example Usage

- **Authorship Analysis**: Input texts and analyze stylistic features to identify potential authorship. For instance, input segments of the "Aeneid" versus the "Metamorphoses" and receive insights into stylistic similarities and differences.

- **Textual Clustering**: Group texts by style and voice, helping researchers uncover relationships and shared characteristics among authors.

### 3.8 Atlas

**Atlas** is an innovative mapping tool allowing users to visualize geographical contexts mentioned in classical texts, providing greater insight into the location of events and characters.

#### Example Usage

- **Geographic Context**: Track Odysseus's journey across the Mediterranean, with annotated maps highlighting significant locations that are linked with specific events in "The Odyssey."

- **Historical Mapping**: Access historical geography of regions mentioned in classical texts to provide context and deepen understanding.

## 5. Troubleshooting

This section addresses common issues and their solutions:

### Issue: Application Won't Start

- **Solution**: Ensure your system meets the minimum requirements mentioned in Section 2.1. If it does, reinstall the application, making sure to close other applications that might conflict.

### Issue: Login Problems

- **Solution**: Try resetting your password through the "Forgot Password?" option. Check your email filters if you do not receive the email.

### Issue: Slow Performance

- **Solution**: Clear the cache of your browser if you are using the web-based version. Ensure you have a stable internet connection.

### Issue: Semantic Analysis Error

- **Solution**: Ensure the input text is correctly formatted. Double-check for special characters that may be affecting the software's processing ability.

### Issue: Translation Not Displaying

- **Solution**: Verify internet connectivity, as the translation feature relies on external databases. Refresh the page and reload the text.
