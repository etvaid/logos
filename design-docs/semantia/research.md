# SEMANTIA: Competitive Research Analysis

## 1. COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

### What existing tools do well (MUST MATCH):
- **Speed & Reliability**: Logeion's instant lookup, TLG's comprehensive search
- **Authority**: Perseus's canonical citations, TLG's scholarly credibility
- **Coverage**: TLG's 110M+ words, Perseus's cross-referenced corpus
- **Morphological awareness**: All tools handle inflected forms
- **Free access model**: Perseus/Logeion prove scholars expect this

### Critical gaps to exploit:
- **Context collapse**: All tools show isolated examples, not semantic neighborhoods
- **Static definitions**: Dictionary meanings frozen in time, no usage evolution
- **Manual bottleneck**: Scholars must know what to look for before searching
- **Meaning blindness**: Tools find words but not concepts/ideas
- **Artificial boundaries**: Tools separate authors/periods instead of tracking meaning flows

### Scholar pain points nobody addresses:
- "I know this word means X in Homer, but what does it really mean in Plato?"
- "Show me all the ways authors talk about 'justice' without using δικαιοσύνη"
- "I want to see semantic innovation happening across generations"
- "Find passages where words are used in surprisingly similar ways"

## 2. INNOVATION OPPORTUNITIES

### "FINALLY!" features:
- **Semantic neighborhoods**: See words that appear in similar contexts automatically
- **Meaning drift visualization**: Watch concepts evolve across centuries
- **Cross-author concept mapping**: Find how different writers express same ideas
- **Contextual similarity search**: "Find passages like this one" regardless of exact words
- **Emergent meaning detection**: Spot when familiar words develop new senses

### Now-possible technology:
- **Large language models**: Can understand semantic similarity across texts
- **Vector embeddings**: Represent meaning mathematically for clustering
- **Graph databases**: Model complex relationships between words/concepts/texts
- **Real-time computation**: No more waiting for complex searches
- **Interactive visualization**: Explore meaning spaces dynamically

### AI advantages over manual tools:
- Process entire corpus simultaneously for patterns
- Find non-obvious connections between distant texts
- Cluster meanings without predefined categories
- Surface rare but significant usage patterns
- Adapt to user's research questions dynamically

- **Citation integration**: Connect findings to existing scholarship
- **Collaboration**: Share discoveries with colleagues/students
- **Authoritative backing**: Results must be defensible in peer review

### Teachers:
- **Student engagement**: Make ancient texts feel alive and connected
- **Concept illustration**: Show how ideas develop and spread
- **Assignment creation**: Find good examples for specific pedagogical points
- **Accessibility**: Complex ideas made visible and understandable
- **Classroom ready**: Export findings into teaching materials

## 4. TECHNICAL APPROACH

### Essential data layers:
- **Full text corpus**: All major Greek/Latin works with canonical citations
- **Morphological analysis**: Lemmatized forms linked to surface words
- **Translation alignments**: Connect original language to English concepts
- **Metadata richness**: Author, date, genre, literary context for each passage
- **Semantic vectors**: Mathematical representations of meaning for each word occurrence

### AI capabilities to implement:
- **Contextual embeddings**: BERT-style models trained on classical texts
- **Clustering algorithms**: Group semantically similar word uses
- **Similarity search**: Find passages expressing similar concepts
- **Anomaly detection**: Spot unusual or innovative word usage
- **Graph neural networks**: Model relationships between words, authors, concepts

### High-impact visualizations:
- **Semantic clusters**: Interactive word clouds showing meaning neighborhoods
- **Evolution timelines**: How word meanings shift across authors/periods
- **Concept networks**: Graph showing how ideas connect across texts
- **Usage heatmaps**: Intensity of semantic relationships across corpus
- **Comparative views**: Side-by-side meaning analysis between authors
