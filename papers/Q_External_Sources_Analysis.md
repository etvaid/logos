## 2. Didache: Q's Two Ways and Lord's Prayer

```
Our Father who art in heaven, hallowed be Thy name.
Thy kingdom come.
Thy will be done on earth, as it is in heaven.
Give us today our daily bread,
and forgive us our debt as we also forgive our debtors.
And bring us not into temptation,

for Thine is the power and the glory for ever.
```

## 7. Implications for Q Reconstruction

### 7.1 The Gospel of Thomas as Q Witness

Thomas shares approximately **35-40%** of its sayings with Q material, making it the most important external witness to the Q tradition. Several observations:

1. **Thomas often preserves shorter forms** — suggesting earlier versions before Matthean/Lukan expansion
2. **Thomas lacks Q's apocalyptic framing** — supporting Kloppenborg's view that Q^1 (sapiential) preceded Q^2 (apocalyptic)
3. **Thomas may preserve Q material lost from Mt/Lk** — especially wisdom sayings that didn't fit their narrative frameworks

### 7.2 Revised Q Word Count

If we include high-confidence lost Q material from external sources:

| Component | Word Count |
|-----------|------------|
| Traditional Q (Mt/Lk double tradition) | ~4,500 |
| High-confidence external additions | ~200 |
| Medium-confidence additions | ~400 |
| **Expanded Q estimate** | **~5,100 words** |

### 7.3 Theological Implications

The external sources strengthen the picture of Q as:

1. **Primarily a wisdom collection** — focus on Jesus' teaching, not passion
2. **Community instruction manual** — ethical teaching for community life (cf. Didache)
3. **Prophetic oracle collection** — judgment sayings, eschatological warnings
4. **Proto-Gnostic tendencies** — Thomas's "hidden sayings" formula may reflect Q's self-understanding

## 8. Conclusion

The external sources—particularly the Gospel of Thomas and Didache—provide crucial evidence for understanding and potentially expanding the Q Source. While the canonical double tradition remains our primary evidence, these texts:

1. **Confirm Q's existence** as an independent sayings tradition
2. **Preserve alternative versions** that may be more original
3. **Contain material that may have been in Q** but was not used by Matthew or Luke
4. **Illuminate Q's theological profile** and community context
